Language Jutsu — unsigned Mac installer

1. Open “Install Language Jutsu”.
2. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to
   the blocked-app message, and choose Open Anyway. Confirm when prompted.
3. Click Install. The speech model downloads automatically (about 1.5 GiB).
4. Follow the completion screen to start a lesson in Codex or Claude Code.

This download is not signed with an Apple Developer ID or notarized by Apple.

You can eject this disk image after setup finishes. Lessons and the installed
program remain on your Mac. Run a newer installer to update the program;
your saved lessons and downloaded model are retained.
